AI video ads are short paid videos where the footage, the presenter, the voice or the variations are produced with generative AI instead of a camera. They perform when they follow the same rules as any good social ad: a vertical frame, a clear hook in the first seconds, sound on, the product shown accurately, and enough versions to test. The difference is speed and cost per version, which lets you test far more ideas. To make AI video ads that work on Meta, TikTok and YouTube:

1. **Start from one idea and one product truth**, not from a tool.
2. **Build a master in 9:16**, then adapt to 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.
3. **Write three to five hooks** for the same body and ending.
4. **Respect each platform's specs and safe zones** so text and logos are not covered.
5. **Disclose realistic AI content** where the platform requires it.
6. **Test in rounds**, keep the winners, and regenerate only what lost.

## Choose the right AI video ad format

Each format suits a different job. Pick the format before you pick the tool.

### Product and world films

The product sits in a generated setting, such as a parasol opening on a beach. These run 6 to 15 seconds and need no dialogue. The setting can be generated; the product should come from real references or a 3D model.

### AI UGC and presenter ads

A presenter talks to camera in a creator-like style, useful for testing scripts and offers at volume. The risk: an AI presenter must never claim personal experience with the product. If this is your main format, see how we produce [AI UGC ads](/ai-ugc-agency) for testing.

### Animated and stylized ads

2D or 3D animation, mascots and illustrated worlds. Character consistency needs a reference set and manual fixes.

### Cutdowns of a hero film

One brand film cut into 6, 10 and 15 second versions, often the most efficient use of AI generated video ads in a campaign that also runs on TV or CTV.

## Write hooks for the first seconds

The opening carries the ad. YouTube's Shorts guidance notes that the call-to-action button appears after 3 seconds in Performance Max, App and Demand Gen campaigns, which gives you a short window to earn attention before you ask for a click.

### Hook patterns that suit AI

- **The impossible image:** something a camera could not capture, shown in the first frame.
- **The transformation:** an everyday object turns into the product or the destination.
- **The question or claim on screen:** a short text overlay inside the safe zone.
- **The direct address:** a presenter who speaks within the first second.

### Keep sound on

Google states in its Shorts ads guide that sound has been shown to increase conversions by over 20%, and recommends music, voiceover or both. Generate or license a track that fits the pace of your cut, and add captions for viewers who watch muted.

## Platform specs for AI video ads

Specs change often. The table below reflects each platform's documentation as checked in November 2026; confirm in Ads Manager before final export.

| Platform and placement | Aspect ratio | Resolution | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta: Instagram and Facebook Reels ads | 9:16 | 1440 x 2560 recommended (1080 x 1920 common) | Instagram Reels ads up to 15 minutes | Keep text out of the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% on each side; MP4 or MOV, up to 4 GB |
| Meta: Facebook feed video | 4:5 recommended | 1440 x 1800 recommended | 1 second up to 241 minutes | MP4, MOV or GIF, up to 4 GB |
| TikTok: auction In-Feed ads | 9:16 recommended (16:9 and 1:1 accepted) | At least 540 x 960 for 9:16 | Up to 10 minutes | Up to 500 MB; bitrate at least 516 kbps; use TikTok's safe zone file |
| YouTube: Shorts ads | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | 6 to 60 seconds (Video reach); under 60 seconds recommended | Only the first 60 seconds play in the Shorts feed |
| YouTube: skippable in-stream | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | 15 seconds recommended | Upload as public or unlisted YouTube video |
| YouTube: non-skippable in-stream | 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 | 1080p recommended, 720p minimum | 7 to 15 seconds (30 seconds on CTV, horizontal only) | Bumpers are up to 6 seconds |

Meta figures are from Sprout Social's October 2026 spec guide, which cites Meta's own guidance; TikTok and YouTube figures come from the platforms' help centers.

### Build for the safe zone first

On Reels, the interface covers a large share of the frame. Plan text and logos for the center of the 9:16 frame, and generate with margin around the subject so you can reframe to 4:5 and 1:1.

## Version and test your AI video ads

Versioning is where an AI video ad maker or an AI studio earns its cost. Plan the test before you produce.

### Build a version matrix

Keep the body and ending fixed and vary one element at a time:

- **Hooks:** three to five openings.
- **Settings:** two or three worlds for the same product.
- **Formats:** 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.
- **Offers and copy:** text overlays and end cards.

### Test in rounds

Launch a first batch, read hook rate, hold rate and cost per result, then cut the losers and produce new variants of the winners. Meta has reported, via eMarketer in 2026, that advertisers in a beta who used AI video generation for most of their campaign ads saw average gains of 10% in click-through rate and 8% in conversion rate. Treat that as a platform claim, not a promise for your account.

### Label what needs labeling

TikTok's Ads Manager includes an AI-generated content disclaimer and states that disclaimers are mandatory for AI-generated, synthetic or manipulated media, including real footage significantly modified by AI. Meta shows an AI disclosure in the "About this ad" menu when it detects AI-made content, as Marketing Dive reported in July 2026. Build the label into your launch checklist.

## AI video ad maker tools vs an agency

| | Self-serve tools | AI video agency |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | High-volume tests, simple product shots, in-house teams with an editor | Brand campaigns, accurate products, consistent characters, multiple markets |
| Speed | Same day for simple edits | Weeks for a finished campaign with versions |
| Control of product accuracy | Limited; depends on your references and skills | 3D, compositing and manual fixes |
| Rights and disclosure | Your responsibility | Shared, defined in the contract |
| Cost model | Subscription and credits | Scoped project fee |

Tools such as Meta's Advantage+ creative features, Runway, Kling, Veo and Higgsfield are good for fast iteration. An agency makes sense when the product must be exact or one idea has to run across many markets. Many teams combine both: an agency builds the master assets, the in-house team iterates. ## FAQ

### What are AI video ads?

AI video ads are paid social or video ads where some or all of the footage, presenter, voice or music is generated with AI. They follow the same rules as filmed ads, with a lower cost per version to test.

### Which aspect ratio should I use for AI ads for social media?

Start with 9:16 vertical, which TikTok recommends for In-Feed ads and YouTube prioritizes for Shorts. Then adapt to 4:5 for Facebook and Instagram feeds and 16:9 for YouTube in-stream.

### Do I have to disclose AI-generated video ads?

On TikTok, disclaimers are mandatory for AI-generated, synthetic or manipulated media, and Meta adds AI disclosures it detects. State laws can add their own rules for synthetic performers. Check the rules for each platform and market before launch.

### How long should an AI video ad be?

Short is usually better on social: YouTube recommends Shorts ads under 60 seconds and suggests 10 to 30 seconds for action-oriented ads. Bumpers are up to 6 seconds and skippable in-stream ads are typically 15 seconds.

### Is an AI video ad maker enough, or do I need an agency?

A self-serve AI video ad maker is enough for fast tests and simple product shots if you have an editor. An agency is worth it when the product must be exact, the characters must stay consistent, or the campaign has to run across many placements and markets.
