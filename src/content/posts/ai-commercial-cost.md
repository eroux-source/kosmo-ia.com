An AI commercial costs anywhere from a few thousand dollars in tool credits for a scrappy social spot to well into six figures for a broadcast campaign, and the gap comes from people, not software. Publicly reported price points give a sense of the range. Kalshi said the AI prompting behind its 2025 NBA Finals spot cost under $2,000, with the director's fee on top and undisclosed. Semafor reported in 2025 that the studio Genre.ai charges around $100,000 for a 30-second commercial while spending only a few thousand dollars on Veo credits, and that the Dor Brothers charge roughly €200,000 (about $235,000) for larger spots. For comparison, Simulmedia's 2025 pricing guide puts a national-standard traditional TV commercial at $50,000 to $500,000. What sets your AI commercial cost:

1. **Creative development:** concept, script and storyboard.
2. **Shot difficulty:** realistic human faces and dialogue cost the most.
3. **Consistency:** recurring characters and exact product accuracy.
4. **Post-production:** edit, compositing, 3D product, grading, sound.
5. **Rights:** music, voice, likeness and any union obligations.
6. **Versions:** lengths, aspect ratios and markets.

The pattern: generation is cheap, and almost all of the budget pays for direction, selection and finishing. Kalshi is the floor for a skilled director on a deliberately chaotic idea; Genre.ai and the Dor Brothers show what established AI studios charge for broadcast work.

## What drives AI video production cost

### Creative development

A clear script and shot list save money later, because every vague shot means hundreds of extra generations. According to NPR, Kalshi's director used AI chat tools to turn the script into shot-by-shot prompts before generating anything.

### Generations and selection

Most AI video clips are not usable. NPR reported that the Kalshi spot needed 300 to 400 generations to get 15 usable clips, and The Wall Street Journal reported that more than 70,000 clips were prompted, generated and refined for one of Coca-Cola's 2025 holiday films. The cost here is mostly the time of the people choosing and fixing shots.

### Faces, dialogue and consistency

Realistic people speaking on camera, and the same character looking identical across shots, are the hardest things to get right. Products are similar: a label, logo or bottle shape that drifts between frames is unusable for a brand. This is where a CGI pass (a 3D model of the product composited into AI footage) often costs less than regenerating shots over and over.

### Post-production and sound

Editing, compositing, grading, sound and music are still human work, and they are what make an AI commercial look like a real commercial.

### Rights, talent and versions

If a real performer's likeness or voice is involved, you pay for it. Under the 2025 SAG-AFTRA commercials contracts, summarized by DLA Piper, using a performer's digital replica requires their consent and payment at 1.5 times the scale session fee per commercial, and synthetic performers appearing alongside a human principal also trigger payments. Each extra length, aspect ratio or market version adds editing time too.

## AI vs traditional production, line by line

The table below uses the line items and ranges from Simulmedia's 2025 guide for the traditional column. The AI column describes what usually replaces each item; actual costs depend on the project.

| Line item | Traditional production (Simulmedia, 2025) | AI production |
|---|---|---|
| Concept development | $5,000 to $50,000 | Same need: strategy and creative still come first |
| Scriptwriting | $1,000 to $20,000 | Same need, plus a detailed prompt and shot list |
| Casting | $500 to $50,000+ | Optional; AI characters, or licensed talent if a real person appears |
| Crew | $10,000 to $100,000+ per day | Replaced by a small team of AI artists over days or weeks |
| Equipment rental | $5,000 to $50,000 | Replaced by software subscriptions and generation credits |
| Location fees | $1,000 to $100,000+ | Generated; no permits or travel |
| Talent fees | $500 to $50,000+ per day | None for synthetic characters; consent and fees for any replica of a real person |
| Editing | $5,000 to $50,000 | Same need, often more time spent on selection |
| Special effects | $10,000 to $100,000+ | Partly replaced; CGI and compositing still used for products and fixes |
| Sound and music | $3,000 to $40,000 | Same need; music and voice must be licensed or cleared |
| Color grading | $2,000 to $10,000 | Same need, to unify shots from different generations |

The savings come from the production day: crew, equipment, locations and travel. Pre-production and post-production stay, and selection can take longer than on a shoot.

## Where AI does not save you money

AI is not automatically cheaper or faster. NBC News reported that the CEO of the production company behind McDonald's Netherlands' 2025 AI Christmas ad said it took more hours than a traditional shoot. Coca-Cola told the WSJ that its AI campaign involved a headcount similar to its non-AI productions, although its CMO said the process was cheaper and faster.

## How to budget an AI commercial

- Write down where it will run (TV, streaming, social) and in which formats before asking for quotes.
- Decide whether real people, a recurring character or exact product shots are needed. These drive the price most.
- Ask what is included: number of revision rounds, cutdowns, music licensing, voice.
- Ask how the product will be kept accurate, and confirm who owns the final files and what the AI tools' terms allow for commercial use.
- Plan disclosure: platforms label realistic synthetic content, and claims about the product must stay truthful.

## FAQ

### How much does an AI commercial cost on average?

There is no reliable average, because few brands disclose budgets. Public figures range from under $2,000 in prompting for Kalshi's spot, according to NPR, to around $100,000 for a 30-second commercial from Genre.ai and roughly €200,000 for larger spots from the Dor Brothers, according to Semafor.

### Is an AI commercial cheaper than a traditional shoot?

Usually, because crew, equipment, locations and travel largely disappear. Simulmedia puts a national-standard traditional TV spot at $50,000 to $500,000. But concept, editing, sound and rights remain, and some AI productions have taken more hours than a shoot.

### How much do AI video tools cost for a commercial?

Software is the smallest line. Semafor reported that Genre.ai spends only a few thousand dollars on Veo credits per spot, and Kalshi said its prompting cost under $2,000. Most of the budget pays for people.

### Do I need to pay actors for an AI commercial?

If you use a real performer's likeness or voice, yes. Under the 2025 SAG-AFTRA commercials contracts, digital replicas require consent and payment, and synthetic performers alongside human principals also trigger fees.

### How long does an AI commercial take to make?

From two days for Kalshi's spot to around a month for Coca-Cola's holiday campaign, according to NPR and the WSJ. Character-heavy work can take longer: Svedka's Super Bowl character rebuild took about four months, as TechCrunch reported.
